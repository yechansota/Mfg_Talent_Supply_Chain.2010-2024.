NOTE ON THE 'MENTORSHIP VACUUM' PREMISE
Seniors are not scarce relative to juniors at the aggregate level, so
a headcount-based mentoring shortage does not bind. The defensible
claim is that mentor availability per junior changes, not that it
collapses. A binding shortage would have to be demonstrated at
occupation level (SOC), which these industry files cannot support.
The feedback-loop collapse reported in the earlier version is dropped.

==============================================================================
  HEADLINE FINDINGS
==============================================================================
1. Senior share of the workforce rose 21.7% -> 29.0% (+0.54 pp/yr).
2. Replacement Ratio fell 4.77 -> 2.71; crosses 1.0 in 2033 (95% CI 2031-2037).
3. Manufacturing has the LOWEST separation rate of its peer set (30.8% annualised).
4. Retirement's share of senior separations fell 81.2% -> 65.5%; job-to-job exits grew +257%.
5. 31% of separations move to another manufacturing employer in the same region.

==============================================================================
  SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
==============================================================================
- J2J Origin-Destination covers moves with both endpoints inside the five
-   states. Out-of-region moves are not observed.
- ENPersist proxies retirement only for the 55+ group.
- State-level aggregation masks county and corridor variation.
- 2020-2021 are shaded in every figure and excluded from calibration.
- Layer 4 is withheld when it fails its backtest. See the log for the
-   diagnosis; a failing projection is reported as a failure, not shown.
- Association only. No causal identification is claimed.
